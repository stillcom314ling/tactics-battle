Phone Cast for Kobo

Send your phone's screen to your Kobo.

One file. Drop it on the device, wait for the reboot, done.

Download KoboRoot.tgz KOReader and Phone Cast, ready to run.

Install

You need a USB cable that carries data, not only power.

  1. Download KoboRoot.tgz above.
  2. Plug the Kobo into a computer. Tap Connect on the Kobo when it asks.
  3. Open the drive named KOBO. Show hidden files, then open the folder .kobo.
  4. Copy KoboRoot.tgz into .kobo. Do not unzip it, do not rename it.
  5. Eject the drive safely, then unplug the cable.
  6. The Kobo reboots and installs itself. Give it a minute. It restarts into KOReader.

Hidden files: Windows — View → Show → Hidden items. macOS — ⌘⇧. in Finder.

Then, from your phone

  1. On the Kobo: the wrench menu → Phone Cast → Start listening.
  2. It brings Wi-Fi up and shows a URL like http://192.168.1.50:8080.
  3. Open that URL on your phone and use the upload form. The image appears on the Kobo.

That form is the whole phone side — nothing to install. If you'd rather push a screenshot without opening a browser, point Tasker's HTTP Request action or an iOS Shortcut at /upload: POST, the image as the raw body, Content-Type: image/png. Give the Kobo a DHCP reservation first, or its address will move.

Why the cable

KoboRoot.tgz has to land in /mnt/onboard/.kobo/ on internal storage, and stock Kobo firmware runs nothing that will accept a file over the network. An external microSD card doesn't help either, because that folder isn't on it — so there is no wireless install, and looking for one costs an hour.

Already have KOReader?

Take the plugin on its own. It's built here in the browser, so the settings you pick are baked into the file you get. Same install: copy it into .kobo, eject, reboot.

Writes phonecast.koplugin and nothing else.

If something goes wrong

The Kobo never reboots after ejecting.
The file landed somewhere other than .kobo, or the cable is power-only. Both look identical from the outside.
No Phone Cast entry in the menu.
KOReader is running but didn't load the plugin. Restart it: top menu → Exit → Restart.
Nothing happens when the phone uploads.
Phone and Kobo are on different networks, or the router isolates clients on its guest network.
It stops working after a few minutes.
The Kobo suspended and took Wi-Fi with it. Press the power button before sending, or raise the sleep timeout in KOReader's power settings.

What's in the file

Upstream KOReader and KFMon exactly as they ship, plus phonecast.koplugin. Your books and settings are never written to.

KFMon is the launcher — the standard way KOReader starts on a Kobo. It is the only part that lands outside .adds/: it adds /usr/local/kfmon/ and a udev rule, and it replaces one firmware script, /etc/init.d/on-animator.sh. Nothing else in the firmware is touched, and .kobo/ is never written to. Resolved upstream versions are listed on each release.
