# Orbfall — balance log

Anchors follow the P&D era table (act-1 ≈ 2012-13 era): team HP ~13K,
enemy hits 1.6-3K, enemy HP 16-26K (x1+0.22/floor), elite 60-70K,
boss 300K. Player chip turn ~8K, condition-met turn 30-60K, committed
burst 100K+.

- Units ATK 900-1750, HP 2600-5000, RCV 100-500: novice 2-combo turns
  (~8K) kill floor-1 enemies in 2-3 turns while taking ~15% HP/turn.
- Leaders capped 3.5-6.5x single-lead (early-era single-lead range).
- Boss void at 80K: an on-condition burst trips it, forcing either
  controlled damage or the Voidpiercer 3x3 answer; resolve at 30% punishes
  sloppy lethal, countered by fixed nuke / gravity / multi-unit turns.
- Elite combo shield at 4: reachable with one skyfall on a 3-combo plan.
- Headless smoke runs (random drags = worse than novice) die on floors
  2-5: acceptable floor for random play; not yet hand-validated on device.
